## ✨ What is FitFlow AI?

FitFlow AI helps you keep track of your daily fitness and wellness journey.

It can create personalized workout schedules and daily tasks based on your goals, routine, and preferences.

## 🚀 Features

- 🏋️ Workout & exercise tracking
- 🚶 Activity & steps
- 💧 Hydration
- 🍎 Meal tracking
- 😴 Sleep & recovery
- ✅ Daily habits
- 📊 Progress tracking
- 🤖 AI-generated plans
- 💬 AI Coach

## 🛠️ Built With

`React` `TypeScript` `Firebase` `Firestore` `Gemini AI`
